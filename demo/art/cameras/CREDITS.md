# Camera footage

Real, free footage from Wikimedia Commons, for the demo's cameras. No private homes and no faces: the people on Street are
small figures across a boulevard, and the number plates on Driveway are blurred.

Every clip was changed the same way: trimmed, stabilised to a fixed view and cropped a little, cross-faded so it loops
(Driveway needs no fade: it starts and ends empty), scaled to 720p at 20 fps, stripped of sound and re-encoded as H.264.
Stills and the `ev-*.jpg` thumbnails are frames from these clips.
