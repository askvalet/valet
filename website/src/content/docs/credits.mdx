## Libraries

| Project | Used for |
|---|---|
| [Hono](https://hono.dev) | The server. |
| [React](https://react.dev) | The page. |
| [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com) | Styles. |
| [Radix UI](https://www.radix-ui.com) | Dialogs, sliders, tooltips. |
| [cmdk](https://cmdk.paco.me) | The command palette. |
| [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) | Icons. |
| [AI SDK](https://ai-sdk.dev) | The model connection and MCP. |
| [Zod](https://zod.dev) | The config schema. |
| [yaml](https://eemeli.org/yaml/) | Reading `config.yaml`. |
| [thinking-orbs](https://www.npmjs.com/package/thinking-orbs) | The chat's working states and the recap's loading. MIT. |
| [border-beam](https://www.npmjs.com/package/border-beam) | The light around the chat box, if you set `chat.beam`, and around an approval card. MIT. |
| [Blossom Carousel](https://www.blossom-carousel.com) | Every carousel: rows of artwork, `focus`, `coverflow`, `masonry`, `cards` and the cameras' events. Native scrolling and snapping, and throwing with a mouse. MIT. |
| [Motion](https://motion.dev) | The springs: a tile flipping open, the chat arriving and being thrown away, a camera growing to full screen, a note swiped off and the tab pill sliding across. Loaded when the page is idle, never on the first load. MIT. |

`thinking-orbs` and `border-beam` come from libraries.dev and are pinned to exact versions.

The feel of rows and counters was rewritten from Arc UI's free MIT items. Nothing is imported from it.

Liquid glass follows kubai087's [glasswork](https://github.com/kubai087/glasswork) (MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Kubai087): its blur, tone, lit rim and bevel. The licence is in `NOTICE`.

## This site

Built with [Astro](https://astro.build) and [Starlight](https://starlight.astro.build), and searched with [Pagefind](https://pagefind.app), all of it static and self-contained. The screenshots are Valet's real renderers, drawn from made-up data.

## Demo pictures

Every name in the screenshots is invented, and posters and artwork are drawn in code. The documents are made-up letters. The camera footage, landscape and meal photos are real, free works, resized and changed for the demo.

## The demo

The [demo](https://demo.askvalet.dev) runs on a made-up homelab, and its pictures come from these places:

- Film and TV art and data come from [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
- Book covers come from [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org).
- Game box art comes from [libretro-thumbnails](https://github.com/libretro-thumbnails).
- Album art in the year in review comes from Apple Music. Navidrome's album covers come from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org), CC0, CC BY-SA 3.0 or public domain, credited above.
- Wallpaper and album photos come from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com).
- Landscape photos come from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org): Lake Louise (CC BY-SA 4.0), Isle of Skye (CC BY-SA 3.0), Torres del Paine and Moraine Lake.
- Meal photos come from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org), CC0 or CC BY 2.0, credited above.
- Saved page previews are screenshots of the pages they link to. Wikipedia text is CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Camera footage is free stock video, credited in the repository under `demo/art/cameras`.

Film, book, game and album art belongs to its owners. It's shown only to illustrate the demo, and any of it can be removed on request.
